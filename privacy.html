Zero-Data Policy

Privacy Policy for Rect10

Effective Date: September 19, 2026

Executive Summary: Rect10 does not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal information, analytics, telemetry, or device identifiers. The application operates 100% locally and offline on your device.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to the Rect10 mobile application ("the App"), developed by JJtan2002 ("Developer", "we", "our"). We believe your privacy is fundamental. This policy explains our practices regarding user data when you play Rect10.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

Rect10 is engineered with zero telemetry. Specifically, we do not collect:

  • Personal Identifiable Information (PII): No names, email addresses, phone numbers, usernames, or physical locations.
  • Device Identifiers: No Android Advertising IDs (AAID), IMEI numbers, MAC addresses, or hardware fingerprints.
  • Analytics & Usage Telemetry: No tracking of play duration, tap events, retention metrics, or error telemetry.
  • Financial Information: Rect10 is completely free with zero in-app purchases and zero advertisements.

3. How Local Data is Stored

The App maintains game state solely on your device via standard local browser storage (localStorage). This data includes:

  • High scores across board sizes (All-Time, Weekly, and Daily bests).
  • Recent game performance history (scores, clears, ranks).
  • Career score progression towards tactical skill unlocks (Clairvoyance, Gravity, Reroll).
  • User preferences (sound volume mute and haptic feedback toggles).

This data never leaves your device. It is never transmitted across the internet, backed up to external servers, or accessible to third parties. If you uninstall the app or clear its application storage, this local data is erased.

4. Third-Party Services and SDKs

Rect10 contains no third-party advertising SDKs, tracking frameworks, or analytics services (such as Google AdMob, Unity Ads, Firebase Analytics, or Facebook SDK).

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Rect10 is suitable for all ages, including children under 13. Because the App collects no personal information whatsoever, it is fully compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play's Families Policy requirements.

6. Permissions

The App requires no sensitive device permissions. It does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, location, or external storage.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may occasionally update this Privacy Policy. Any updates will be posted to the official repository at https://github.com/JJtan2002/rect10.
