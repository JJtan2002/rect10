## 2. Full Description (Max 4,000 characters)

```text
Draw rectangles. Sum to 10. Clear the board.

Rect10 is a sleek, fast-paced arithmetic puzzle game designed for pure mental flow. Scan an organized grid of digits (1–9), drag a box around any rectangular region whose numbers add up to exactly 10, and release to clear them!

Whether you are connecting a pair (7 + 3), a trio (5 + 3 + 2), or an expansive multi-cell block (1 + 2 + 3 + 4), any rectangular shape works. Cleared tiles vanish, allowing you to tunnel across empty spaces to unlock high-scoring combos!

⚡ GAME MODES FOR EVERY MOOD
• 100s Challenge: Race against the 100-second clock! Maintain your clearing pace, earn rank titles from Novice to Grandmaster, and log your best scores to daily, weekly, and all-time leaderboards.
• Zen Free Mode: No clock, no pressure. Play at your own leisurely pace until all possible moves are exhausted. Perfect for winding down or mastering advanced tactical tunneling.

🧩 3 DYNAMIC BOARD SIZES
• Small (7 × 10): Fast, tight board ideal for quick 1-minute brain workouts on the go.
• Medium (9 × 12): Balanced standard grid featuring expansive combination possibilities.
• Large (11 × 15): The ultimate 165-cell arithmetic canvas for puzzle masters.

📜 MISSIONS & TACTICAL POWERS
Rack up career points in Challenge Mode to unlock game-changing tactical powers:
• 👁️ Clairvoyance (100k pts): Instantly scans the board and highlights a guaranteed valid match.
• ⬇️ Gravity (200k pts): Drops active numbers downward to collapse empty gaps at the bottom.
• 🎲 Reroll (300k pts): Re-rolls all remaining numbers with fresh digits to break deadlocks.
Each skill can be used once per round to turn the tide of a high-score attempt!

✨ PURE TACTILE SATISFACTION
• Responsive Touch: Ultra-crisp High-DPI Canvas 2D rendering optimized for high-refresh 120Hz displays.
• Procedural Web Audio: Dynamic chime chords that scale upward with larger multi-tile clears.
• Haptic Feedback: Subtle, satisfying haptic ticks on every drag step and board clear.
• Detailed Analytics: Review your Clears-Per-Minute (CPM) pace, largest clear size, and cell-size breakdown after every round.

🛡️ ZERO ADS. ZERO TRACKING. 100% OFFLINE.
• No Advertisements: Never interrupted by banners, popups, or rewarded video ads.
• No In-App Purchases: Completely free with zero paywalls.
• Complete Privacy: No personal data collection, no account required, no analytics SDKs.
• Fully Offline: Play anytime, anywhere without an internet connection. High scores and career milestones are stored strictly on your local device.

Sharpen your mental arithmetic and experience the satisfying rhythm of Rect10 today!
```
